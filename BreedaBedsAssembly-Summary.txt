================================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY, TOOL LIST & STEP-BY-STEP ASSEMBLY GUIDE
PROJECT: BREEDA BEDS MURPHY BED ASSEMBLY
================================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & OVERVIEW
-------------------------------
This project details the complete end-to-end assembly procedure for a Breeda Beds wall bed system with side hutches. (Note: Trailing audio bleed-over from the showroom walkthrough video has been omitted to focus 100% on the assembly video).

Key highlights include:
• Bed Frame Capacity: Heavy-duty metal subframe rated up to 1,400 lbs capacity across Twin, Full, and Queen sizes.
• Counter-Balance Spring Mechanism: Adjustable spring tension tuned to mattress weight for effortless one-handed opening and closing.
• Critical 1/2-Inch Reveal Dimension: Precise 0.5" offset required when mounting the metal bed frame onto door face panels to ensure flawless cabinet closure.
• Safety Wall Anchoring: Unit MUST be anchored directly into wall studs via top and bottom nailers before cocking spring arms or lowering bed face.


2. REQUIRED TOOLS & HARDWARE LIST
----------------------------------
[A] Required Tools:
  • Stud Finder (for locating metal wall studs behind drywall)
  • Lipstick / Grease Pencil / Transfer Marking Paste (put lipstick on stud centers to transfer-mark fascia / top nailer board)
  • Power Drill / Driver with:
    - 1/2" Drill Bit (for 1/4"-20 SnapToggle anchor holes into metal studs)
    - 1/8" Drill Bit (for pilot holes into studs)
    - 3/8" Drill Bit or Countersink Bit (for countersinking bolt heads)
  • Allen Wrench / Hex Keys (for frame bolts)
  • Wrench / Socket Set:
    - 5/16" Wrench/Socket (for 5/16" Nylock nuts on mechanism & frame)
    - 1/4" Wrench/Socket (for leg tie-rod bolts)
  • Screwdrivers (Phillips & Flathead)
  • Rubber Mallet (for tapping cam fittings & leg hardware)
  • PVC Arm Tension Tube (Included helper tube for cocking mechanism arms)
  • Tape Measure / Precision Ruler (Critical for 1/2" header reveal offset)
  • Spirit Level & Shims (for leveling cabinet prior to wall attachment)
  • Spray Lubricant (WD-40 or Silicone Spray - for spring noise)

[B] Hardware Package Components:
  • 1/4"-20 SnapToggle (Toggler) Anchors & 3" x 1/4"-20 Machine Bolts (for metal studs; ~2" grip depth; see https://outline.breda.co/s/walls#h-steel-studs)
  • Cam fittings & Cam connecting studs
  • Fast Caps (decorative screw cover caps)
  • Heavy-duty Counter-Balance Mechanism Springs
  • Hardware Card 3 (Bed face frame 4-corner connectors)
  • Hardware Card 7 (Metal bed frame to door panel screws)
  • Stop Cylinders (inserted into lower corner bracket holes)
  • Allen Head Bolts & 5/16" Nylock Nuts (Hole 1 stop bolt at 6-5/8" from header)
  • 5/16" Hex Head Bolts & Nylock Nuts (Hole 3 mechanism seating bolt at 19-1/4" from header, & Hole 2 security bolt)
  • Nylon washers, steel filler washers, steel holding washers, & Nylock nuts (Leg pivot assembly)
  • Steel Leg Tie-Rod with 1/4" bolts & star washers
  • Cabinet Wall Anchoring Screws & SnapToggle Bolts (Top & bottom nailers)
  • Drawer Slide Hardware & Cam Fittings
  • 35mm Euro Door Hinges & Base Plates (Hole #2 mounting)


3. ORDERED STEP-BY-STEP ASSEMBLY INSTRUCTIONS
----------------------------------------------

--- STAGE 1: CABINET & SIDE HUTCH PREPARATION ---
1. Hardware Pre-Insertion: Lay out cabinet and side hutch panels flat on a soft surface; press cam fittings and thread cam connecting studs into pre-bored holes.
2. Hutch Assembly: Align hutch side panels with top/bottom panels; turn cam fittings clockwise to lock hutch units together.
3. Outer Cabinet Assembly: Position side hutches ~16" from wall. Attach top nailer to bed cabinet top panel. Secure top assembly and toe kick to right hutch, then align left hutch into position and lock all cams.
4. Headboard & Lower Nailer: Insert headboard and lower nailer panel with cam fittings facing upward; tighten cams securely.

--- STAGE 2: LEVELING, FASCIA MARKING & METAL STUD ANCHORING ---
5. Position & Level Cabinet: Tilt bed cabinet upright into final position against wall. Check level side-to-side and front-to-back; slide shims underneath cabinet feet if necessary.
6. Locate Studs & Lipstick Transfer-Mark Fascia: Locate metal studs behind top nailer with stud finder. Put a dab of lipstick on stud center marks on wall, then press top nailer/fascia board flush to transfer exact drill marks onto back of board.
7. Anchor into Metal Studs: Drill 1/2" holes through drywall & metal stud face at marks; install 1/4"-20 SnapToggle anchors into metal studs. Drill countersunk holes through nailer and drive 3" x 1/4"-20 machine bolts (~2" grip depth). Cover bolt heads with Fast Caps. (Ref: https://outline.breda.co/s/walls#h-steel-studs).

--- STAGE 3: MECHANISM & ARM COCKING ---
8. Install Springs: Consult spring application chart for your bed size and mattress weight; insert required number of counter-balance springs onto mechanism plate.
9. Attach Mechanism: Bolt spring mechanism plate to inside face of cabinet side panels using dedicated hardware tube screws.
10. Cock Mechanism Arms: Slip provided PVC tube over spring arm; pull arm downward until internal arm lock clicks into open/cocked position. Repeat for opposite side. (WARNING: High spring tension!).

--- STAGE 4: BED FACE FRAME & DOOR PANELS ---
11. Assemble Bed Frame: Lay steel frame channels on floor; connect 4 corners using Hardware Card 3. Insert stop cylinders into lower bracket holes on the two corners farthest from cabinet.
12. Align Door Panels: Lay door panels face-down on a soft surface with handle holes farthest from cabinet. Press doors tightly together along center seam.
13. Frame-to-Door Placement (CRITICAL STEP): Position metal frame over doors. Set frame exactly 1/2 INCH (0.5") from header end of door panels. (CRITICAL: 1/2" reveal offset!). Drive screws from Hardware Card 7 to secure frame to doors.
14. Support Stiffeners: Attach middle support stiffener midway between header and footer; install remaining two support stiffeners between center stiffener and bed ends.
15. Mechanism Pivot Bolts:
    - Insert Allen head bolt into Hole 1 (6-5/8" from header) and secure with 5/16" Nylock nut (acts as rotation stop).
    - Insert 5/16" Hex head bolts through Hole 3 (19-1/4" from header) from outside; finger-tighten 5/16" Nylock nuts.

--- STAGE 5: MOUNTING BED FACE & LEG SETUP ---
16. Mount Face Assembly to Arms: Stand bed face panel vertically between mechanism arms. Lift assembly and seat Hole 3 hex bolts into notches at end of mechanism arms.
17. Rotate & Secure: Rotate bed face outward until Hole 1 Allen bolt rests against mechanism notch. Insert 5/16" hex bolt into Hole 2; tighten all nuts at Hole 2 and Hole 3 securely. Attach handles.
18. Release Arm Locks: Pull outward on bed face panel to disengage mechanism arm locks (audible click). (Note: Tension will feel heavy until mattress is added).
19. Install Legs & Tie Rod: Slide nylon washer onto leg stem; insert through frame hole. From inside, install steel filler washer, steel holding washer, and Nylock nut. Rotate leg down against stop; connect steel leg tie rod between both legs using 1/4" bolts and star washers.
20. Align Reveals & Bottom Nailer: Close bed face assembly while pivoting legs closed. Inspect side reveals for even spacing. Nudge cabinet base slightly if needed. Attach bottom nailer to wall studs with 1 screw per side.
21. Spring Lubrication (If Noisy): Spray WD-40 or silicone lubricant onto spring coils if noisy during first operation; open and close bed several times to work lube in.

--- STAGE 6: SIDE HUTCH DRAWERS & DOORS ---
22. Drawer Box Assembly: Install cam fittings into drawer panels; assemble sides to back; slide bottom into grooves; attach drawer front; install handles.
23. Drawer Slides: Mount drawer slides to bottom edge of drawer box pushing firmly against back of front face; mount cabinet slide tracks into pre-bored hutch holes; slide drawers into place.
24. Hutch Cabinet Doors: Press hinge base plates into outside panel holes #2; seat 35mm hinges into door back holes and turn screws 1/2 turn clockwise; snap hinges onto base plates; install door handles.
