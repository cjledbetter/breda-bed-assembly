Key Highlights & Features:

  • Heavy-Duty Capacity: Engineered metal subframe structure rated up to 1,400 lbs across Twin, Full, and Queen size variants.
  • Counter-Balance Spring System: High-tension spring mechanisms tuned precisely to mattress weight for effortless one-handed opening and closing.
  • Precision 1/2" Header Reveal Offset: Precise 0.5" offset required when attaching the metal bed subframe onto the door face panels for seamless cabinet closure.
  • Mandatory Structural Wall Anchoring: Cabinet MUST be securely fastened into structural wall studs via top and bottom nailers before cocking tension arms or mounting bed doors.

4. Critical Safety Warnings & Assembly Rules

⚠️ HIGH TENSION MECHANISM ARMS

The spring mechanism arms operate under extreme mechanical tension once cocked. NEVER attempt to pull down or trip spring mechanism arms without using the provided PVC helper tube and maintaining a firm grip. Keep hands clear of mechanism pivot points.

🏗️ MANDATORY STRUCTURAL WALL ANCHORING & STEEL STUD NOTICE

Do NOT cock the spring arms or mount the bed face assembly until the cabinet is securely leveled and anchored to wall studs via both top and bottom nailers.

We have metal studs. Standard wood screws will not hold in light-gauge metal studs under tension. Use 1/4"-20 SnapToggle / Toggler Anchors with 3-inch x 1/4"-20 machine bolts to secure top and bottom nailers through the ~1.5" to 2" grip stack-up into 3-5/8" metal studs. Refer to the official Breda Beds Steel Stud Installation Guide.

📐 CRITICAL 1/2" REVEAL OFFSET

When securing the metal frame to the rear face of the door panels, you MUST measure and set a precise 1/2-inch (0.5") reveal offset from the header edge. Imprecise alignment will prevent cabinet closure and bind door operation.

5. Step-by-Step Assembly Instructions

graph TD A[Stage 1: Cabinet & Side Hutch Prep] --> B[Stage 2: Leveling, Fascia Marking & Metal Stud Anchoring] B --> C[Stage 3: Spring Mechanism & Arm Cocking] C --> D[Stage 4: Bed Frame & Door Panel Assembly] D --> E[Stage 5: Mounting Bed Face & Leg Setup] E --> F[Stage 6: Hutch Drawers & Cabinet Doors]

Stage 1: Cabinet & Side Hutch Preparation

Step 1: Hardware Pre-Insertion

Lay all cabinet and side hutch panels flat on a clean, soft surface (carpet or blanket). Press cam fittings into pre-bored panel holes and thread cam connecting studs flush.

Step 2: Hutch Assembly

Align the hutch side panels with the top and bottom panels. Turn cam fittings clockwise to securely lock the hutch boxes together.

Step 3: Outer Cabinet Assembly

Position the assembled side hutches approximately 16" away from the target wall. Attach the top nailer to the bed cabinet top panel. Secure top assembly and lower toe kick to right hutch, then align left hutch and lock all cams.

Step 4: Headboard & Lower Nailer

Insert the headboard and lower nailer panel with cam fittings facing upward; tighten cams securely to rigidize overall cabinet superstructure.

Stage 2: Leveling, Fascia Marking & Metal Stud Anchoring

Step 5: Position & Level Cabinet

Carefully tilt the assembled bed cabinet upright and move into final position flush against the wall. Use a spirit level to verify level side-to-side and front-to-back. Slide wood shims underneath feet if necessary.

Step 6: Locate Studs & Lipstick Transfer-Mark Fascia Board

Use a magnetic stud finder to locate metal wall studs behind the top nailer board. The Lipstick Trick: Apply a small dab of lipstick (or grease pencil) onto the center points of the located studs on the wall surface. Press the top nailer / fascia board into its exact leveled position against the wall. The lipstick will transfer exact, clean marks onto the back face of the fascia / nailer board, showing precisely where to drill.

Step 7: Drill & Anchor into Metal Studs with 3" SnapToggle Bolts

Drill 1/2" holes through drywall and metal stud face at the marked lipstick locations. Insert 1/4"-20 SnapToggle (Toggler) anchors into metal studs and slide plastic caps flush to drywall. Drill corresponding holes through top nailer board and countersink with a 3/8" bit. Drive 3-inch x 1/4"-20 machine bolts through nailer into SnapToggles (~2" grip depth). Press Fast Caps over bolt heads. (Refer to Breda Steel Stud Guide).

Stage 3: Mechanism Setup & Arm Cocking

Step 8: Install Counter-Balance Springs

Consult spring application table for your bed size and mattress weight; insert designated number of heavy-duty springs onto mechanism backing plates.

Step 9: Attach Mechanism to Cabinet

Mount spring mechanism plate assembly to interior side walls of cabinet using dedicated hardware tube screws.

Step 10: Cock Mechanism Arms

Slip provided PVC helper tube over end of spring arm. Firmly pull arm downward until internal arm lock engages with an audible CLICK. Repeat for opposite side.

Stage 4: Bed Face Frame & Door Panels

Step 11: Assemble Metal Bed Frame

Lay out heavy-duty steel frame channels on floor; connect 4 corners using Hardware Card 3. Press Stop Cylinders into lower corner bracket holes on the two corners farthest from cabinet.

Step 12: Align Door Panels

Place door panels face-down on a soft surface with handle holes farthest from cabinet. Press doors tightly together along center seam.

Step 13: Frame-to-Door Placement (CRITICAL STEP)

Position assembled metal bed subframe over rear face of door panels. CRITICAL MEASUREMENT: Set subframe edge exactly 1/2 INCH (0.5") back from header end of door panels. Drive mounting screws from Hardware Card 7 to secure frame to doors.

Step 14: Install Support Stiffeners

Attach center support stiffener channel midway between header and footer frame tubes; install remaining two support stiffeners evenly spaced.

Step 15: Install Mechanism Pivot Bolts

Hole 1 (Stop Bolt): Insert Allen head bolt into Hole 1 (6-5/8" from header) and secure with 5/16" Nylock nut. Hole 3 (Seating Bolt): Insert 5/16" Hex head bolt into Hole 3 (19-1/4" from header) from outside; finger-tighten 5/16" Nylock nut.

Stage 5: Mounting Bed Face & Leg Setup

Step 16: Mount Face Assembly to Arms

Lift and position bed face panel assembly vertically between cocked mechanism arms. Seat Hole 3 hex bolts into receiver notches at end of arms.

Step 17: Rotate & Secure Bed Face

Pivot bed face outward until Hole 1 Allen stop bolt rests against mechanism notch. Insert 5/16" Hex bolt into Hole 2; tighten all nuts at Hole 2 & 3 securely. Attach handles.

Step 18: Release Mechanism Arm Locks

Pull outward on top of bed face panel to disengage mechanical arm safety locks (listen for click).

Step 19: Install Legs & Tie-Rod

Assemble pivot legs: Slide nylon washer onto leg stem, pass through frame hole, secure inside with steel filler washer, steel holding washer, and 5/16" Nylock nut. Rotate legs down; connect steel leg tie-rod bar using 1/4" bolts and star washers.

Step 20: Align Reveals & Attach Bottom Nailer

Close bed face assembly while pivoting legs closed. Inspect side reveal gaps for uniformity. Attach lower nailer to wall studs with 1 screw per side.

Step 21: Spring Coil Lubrication

If spring coil noise is heard during initial operation, spray WD-40 or Silicone Lubricant across spring coils and open/close bed 3-5 times.

Stage 6: Side Hutch Drawers & Doors

Step 22: Drawer Box Assembly

Install cam fittings into side drawer panels; assemble sides to back panel; slide bottom into grooves; attach front face and hardware handles.

Step 23: Drawer Slide Track Setup

Mount drawer slide tracks flush to lower edge of drawer box; mount interior slide tracks into pre-bored hutch holes; slide drawers into tracks.

Step 24: Hutch Cabinet Doors

Press hinge base plates into outside panel hole pattern #2; seat 35mm Euro hinges into door cup holes and turn locking screws 1/2 turn clockwise; snap hinges onto base plates and adjust alignment screws.

7. Troubleshooting & Maintenance

Issue / Symptom Possible Cause Corrective Action
Spring Noise / Squeaking Friction between dry spring coils Spray WD-40 or silicone lube on coils and cycle bed 5 times.
Uneven Side Reveals Cabinet base out of square Lightly nudge cabinet base or adjust shims under feet before locking bottom nailer.
Bed Hard to Lower Mattress not yet loaded Heavy initial pull tension is normal until mattress weight is present.
Cabinet Gaps at Top Top nailer screws loose Re-tighten wall anchor SnapToggle bolts directly into metal stud centers.
Door Binds When Closing Reveal offset incorrect Loosen Card 7 screws and re-align frame to exact 0.5" header reveal.
