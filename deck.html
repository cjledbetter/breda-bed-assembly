Slide 1 of 6
Stage 1 of 6

Cabinet & Side Hutch Preparation

  • Step 1: Hardware Pre-Insertion: Lay panels flat on a blanket; press cam fittings into pre-bored holes and thread cam studs flush.
  • Step 2: Hutch Assembly: Join hutch side panels to top/bottom panels; turn cams clockwise to lock.
  • Step 3: Outer Cabinet: Place hutches ~16" from wall. Attach top nailer to bed cabinet top panel and join right & left hutches.
Stage 2 of 6

Cabinet Leveling, Fascia Marking & Metal Stud Anchoring

  • Step 5: Position & Level: Stand cabinet upright against wall. Level side-to-side and front-to-back using wood shims under feet.
  • Step 6: Locate & Lipstick Mark: Find metal studs behind top nailer board with stud finder. Put lipstick on stud center marks on wall, then press top nailer/fascia board flush to transfer exact drill points onto back of board.
  • Step 7: Anchor Metal Studs: Drill 1/2" holes at marks, install 1/4"-20 SnapToggle anchors into metal studs, countersink nailer holes, and drive 3" x 1/4"-20 bolts (~2" grip depth). Cover heads with Fast Caps. See Breda Steel Studs Guide.
Stage 3 of 6

Spring Mechanism Mounting & Arm Cocking

  • Step 8: Install Springs: Load required counter-balance springs onto mechanism backing plate based on bed size & mattress weight.
  • Step 9: Attach Plate: Secure spring mechanism plates to inside side walls using dedicated hardware tube screws.
  • Step 10: Cock Mechanism Arms: Slip PVC helper tube over arm. Pull down firmly until internal arm lock clicks open.
Stage 4 of 6

Bed Frame & Precision 0.5" Reveal Alignment

  • Step 11-12: Frame & Panel Setup: Connect 4 frame corners (Card 3). Lay door panels face-down pressed tightly along center seam.
  • Step 13: CRITICAL 0.5" Reveal Offset: Position metal frame exactly 0.5" from top header end of door panels; drive screws (Card 7).
  • Step 15: Pivot Bolts: Insert Hole 1 stop bolt at 6-5/8" and Hole 3 seating bolt at 19-1/4" from header.
Stage 5 of 6

Mounting Bed Face, Legs & Bottom Nailer

  • Step 16-17: Seat Face Panel: Lift bed face; seat Hole 3 bolts into arm notches; rotate out and secure Hole 2 & 3 nuts tightly.
  • Step 18-19: Leg Tie-Rod: Pull bed face to trip arm safety locks. Install pivot legs and 1/4" steel leg tie-rod bar.
  • Step 20-21: Align Reveals: Check reveal gaps upon closing. Anchor bottom nailer to studs. Apply silicone lube if springs squeak.
Stage 6 of 6

Side Hutch Drawers & Cabinet Doors

  • Step 22-23: Drawer Assembly: Assemble drawer boxes with cams; mount lower drawer slide tracks and insert into hutch.
  • Step 24: Hutch Doors: Press base plates into hole pattern #2; seat 35mm Euro hinges into door cups; snap onto plates.
