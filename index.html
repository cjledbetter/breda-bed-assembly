⚠️ MANDATORY STRUCTURAL WALL ANCHORING

Do NOT cock the spring mechanism arms or lower the bed face assembly until the cabinet is securely leveled and anchored into solid wall studs via both top and bottom nailers. Unanchored cabinets will tip forward violently under spring tension.

📐 CRITICAL 1/2-INCH REVEAL OFFSET

When mounting the steel bed subframe onto the door panels, maintain an exact 0.5" (1/2 inch) offset from the top header edge. Incorrect placement prevents smooth cabinet closure and binds mechanism pivot arms.

🛠️ Ordered Step-by-Step Summary

  1. Stage 1: Cabinet & Hutch Preparation: Pre-insert cam fittings; build side hutch units; assemble main cabinet top/bottom nailers.
  2. Stage 2: Leveling & Wall Anchoring: Stand cabinet upright against wall, level with shims, drill 1/8" pilot / 3/8" countersink holes through top nailer into studs, drive wall screws.
  3. Stage 3: Mechanism Setup: Install counter-balance springs, bolt mechanism plates to interior side walls, cock spring arms downward using PVC helper tube until lock clicks.
  4. Stage 4: Bed Frame & Door Assembly: Assemble 4 corners of metal subframe, align door panels face-down, set exact 0.5" header reveal, secure Card 7 screws, install Hole 1 (6-5/8") & Hole 3 (19-1/4") bolts.
  5. Stage 5: Bed Face Mounting & Leg Setup: Seat Hole 3 bolts into arm notches, tighten Hole 2 & 3 nuts, disengage arm locks, install pivot legs and 1/4" leg tie-rod, adjust side reveals, anchor bottom nailer.
  6. Stage 6: Hutch Drawers & Doors: Assemble drawer boxes and slide tracks; mount Euro hinges into hole pattern #2 for hutch cabinet doors.
