🏗️ STEEL / METAL STUD INSTALLATION NOTICE

We have metal studs. Standard wood screws will not hold in light-gauge metal studs under tension. Use 1/4"-20 SnapToggle / Toggler Anchors with 2-1/2" x 1/4"-20 machine bolts (as recommended by Breda Beds) to secure top and bottom nailers through the ~1.5" to 2" grip stack-up into 3-5/8" metal studs. Refer to the official Breda Beds Steel Stud Installation Guide.

📋 6-Stage Ordered Instructions

  1. Stage 1: Cabinet Prep: Insert cams and studs; build side hutch units and attach top/bottom nailers.
  2. Stage 2: Leveling, Fascia Marking & Metal Stud Anchoring: Stand cabinet upright, level with shims. Put lipstick on stud center marks on wall, press top nailer/fascia board flush to transfer exact drill points onto back of board. Drill holes, install 1/4"-20 SnapToggle anchors into metal studs, and drive 2-1/2" bolts. (See Steel Stud Guide).
  3. Stage 3: Spring Mechanism: Load counter-balance springs, bolt mechanism plates to side walls, cock arms with PVC helper tube.
  4. Stage 4: Bed Frame & Doors: Connect 4 frame corners, lay door panels face-down, set 0.5" header reveal, secure Card 7 screws.
  5. Stage 5: Face Mounting & Legs: Seat Hole 3 bolts into arm notches, tighten hardware, release safety locks, install legs and tie-rod.
  6. Stage 6: Hutch Drawers & Doors: Assemble drawer boxes and slide tracks; mount 35mm Euro hinges onto hole pattern #2.

🎴 Step-by-Step Slide Deck

Stage 1: Cabinet & Side Hutch Prep

Step 1: Pre-insert cam fittings and connecting studs into flat panels.

Step 2: Join hutch side panels to top/bottom panels; turn cams clockwise.

Step 3: Position hutches 16" from wall and secure top nailer assembly.

Stage 2: Leveling & Wall Anchoring

Step 5: Stand cabinet upright and level front-to-back with wood shims.

Step 6: Locate wall studs behind top nailer; drill 1/8" pilot holes.

Step 7: Countersink with 3/8" bit and drive heavy-duty screws into studs.

Stage 3: Spring Mechanism & Arm Cocking

Step 8: Load springs onto mechanism plate according to mattress weight.

Step 9: Bolt mechanism plates securely to interior side walls.

Step 10: Slip PVC helper tube over arm and pull down until internal lock clicks.

Stage 4: 0.5" Header Reveal Offset Alignment

Step 11-12: Connect 4 metal frame corners; lay door panels face-down.

Step 13: Set exact 0.5" reveal offset from top header edge before driving Card 7 screws.

Step 15: Install Hole 1 bolt (6-5/8") & Hole 3 bolt (19-1/4").

Stage 5: Face Mounting, Legs & Bottom Nailer

Step 16-17: Seat Hole 3 bolts into arm notches and tighten Hole 2 & 3 hardware.

Step 18-19: Disengage safety arm locks; install pivot legs and 1/4" tie-rod.

Step 20: Verify reveal symmetry and anchor bottom nailer into wall studs.

Stage 6: Side Hutch Drawers & Doors

Step 22-23: Assemble drawer boxes and slide tracks; insert into hutch.

Step 24: Mount 35mm Euro hinges onto hole pattern #2 for hutch cabinet doors.
